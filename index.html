About this study

The original Three.js water simulation, seen straight down. Drawing styles change the image only.

The original square water mesh fills the viewport. Hairline ripples retain complete wave strokes and fade together. Their motion follows the source simulation's drop response. Switch them off to compare the earlier artistic contours, or choose Original to see the full simulated surface. Caustics control the focused light below the surface.

A heightfield wave model with reflection, refraction and caustics. It does not simulate breaking waves, spray or full fluid flow.

Original work: Evan Wallace. Three.js port: Yong Su. MIT license.
