About this study

The original Three.js water wave simulation, seen straight down.

Open edges absorb outgoing waves instead of sending them back. The surface drawing follows the simulated height and slope. Original shows the same simulated surface and geometry.

Bitmap experiments layer over the existing drawing and caustics. Print experiments can replace strokes with marks or add print texture. Switch experiments off to return to the existing drawing.

Projected caustics are refracted light below the water, so highlights can sit apart from ripple crests. Overhead light reduces the sideways shift. Caustic ripples draws those same projected shapes as ink with the caustic lighting switched off. Align glow to ripples is an artistic surface effect.

Dreamy slows and diffuses; Subtle softens impacts and contrast. They combine with each other and the experiments. C, X and / replay fixed touch paths. Space pauses, and H hides the controls.

A heightfield wave model with refraction and caustics. It does not simulate breaking waves, spray or full fluid flow.
