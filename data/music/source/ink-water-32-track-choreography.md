# Ink Water — 32-Track Choreography Guide

This guide combines full-track signal analysis with authored rain-direction notes. The measurements are not the choreography: they identify structure and candidate moments; the rain score should interpret that structure rather than map every beat to a drop.

## Core rule

**music → authored rain scheduler → existing physical raindrop path → water solver → normals/caustics → Ink Water renderer**

Do not directly deform the surface from audio. Do not make `beat = drop`.
